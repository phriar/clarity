In Their Words

Seven women. One week in Bordeaux. Here's what they said when they got home.

2026 Retreat

Unedited reflections from our inaugural cohort

These women arrived as strangers and left as something more. We're grateful they've shared what the week meant to them.

“
I've done plenty of travel, reflection, and coaching before — but Clarity Uncorked was on a whole different level, and so much more than I'd dared to hope for. An amazing opportunity to be guided through deep personal and professional introspection alongside fascinating women, immersed in the beauty of Bordeaux. The women, the coaching, the countryside, and the food each could have been transformative on their own — together they were exceptional.
Integrator-in-Chief & COO
“
Clarity Uncorked gave me time and permission to work on myself — space to examine my priorities and make sure they're in alignment as I move forward. A wonderful week.
Cambridge Executive MBA Candidate
“
A week's vacation for your soul, with real movement forward for your mind. The first evening apéro — tasting the French vibe while getting to know each other — set the tone for everything that followed. I left ready to move forth boldly.
Francophile & Board Director
“
Clarity Uncorked is a physical and mental journey that pushes you outside your comfort zone, creates real community, and — for me — truly uncorked something. It reconnected me with a sense of adventure and joy, and the simple pleasure of learning something new, especially about yourself. I came in with mild apprehension about whether it would be worth it. I am very satisfied.
Geopolitical and National Security Expert
“
An amazing week with a fantastic group of women in a fabulous location — the perfect mix of culture, food, relaxation, and time for real introspection. You could tell the week was curated with intention, by people who knew exactly what they were doing.
Mid-Career Executive Seeking Change
“
For me, Clarity Uncorked was an exploration of the possible. Inspired by an amazing cohort of accomplished women and immersed in the beauty and richness of French country life, I saw options I'd never before considered for myself. For the first time — perhaps ever — I was thinking with my heart as well as my head.
A Perpetual Student Searching for Joy
“
From my arrival, I stepped into a magical setting where the rooms and gardens created a peaceful space to connect over amazing food and wine. Our introspective times were balanced by discovery outside — hiking and biking through the vineyards, wine tasting with female vintners, learning the rich history of the region. All of it magnified a joy and lightness of being. I have never experienced anything like Clarity Uncorked — and I can't imagine doing this with anyone else.
Retired Executive Embracing Abundance
