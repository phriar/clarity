April 2026

Inside the 2026 Retreat

Six days in a maison de maître outside Bordeaux — vineyards, coaching, and a table that never seemed to empty.

A Week to Remember

The first Clarity Uncorked retreat

In April 2026, a small group of women arrived in Southwest France as strangers and left as friends. Over six days they slowed down, reconnected with themselves, and made room for what's next — all set against the vineyards and golden light of Bordeaux.

Day One

Arrival at the Maison

The week began with welcome drinks on the terrace, a tour of the private maison de maître, and the first long, unhurried dinner together — the kind that sets the tone for everything that follows.

Mornings

Reflection Among the Vineyards

Mornings were spent in coaching circles and quiet one-on-one sessions, with the Bordeaux countryside as a backdrop for honest conversation, journaling, and a fresh look at what's next.

Afternoons

Cultural Immersion

Afternoons were for getting out into the region itself — e-bike rides through the vineyards, a guided wine tasting, a sound bath, and walks through the local villages. See what's included →

Evenings

A Feast for the Senses

Evenings belonged to Chef Michael's table — local, seasonal dishes paired with the region's finest wines, shared over conversation that ran long into the night.
