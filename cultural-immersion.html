More Than a Retreat

Bordeaux, lived not just visited

Clarity isn't only found in stillness — it's also found in motion, in history, and in trying something new. Throughout the week, we step beyond the maison to experience the region the way locals do.

From e-bike rides through the vineyards to a guided sound bath, these moments are woven between the coaching and the meals — a chance to be fully present in your body and in Bordeaux itself.

On Two Wheels

Bike Rides Through the Vineyards

One of the week's most joyful mornings is spent on e-bikes, riding through the vineyards and along the river that surrounds the maison. No experience needed — just an easy, guided ride through some of the most beautiful countryside in France.

It's a chance to feel the scale of the landscape that's been growing the wine on your table all week — and to laugh together along the way.

In the Heart of Bordeaux

Wine History & Culture

You're staying in one of the world's most celebrated wine regions — so we make time to actually understand it. Guided tastings introduce the appellations, the terroir, and the centuries of history behind what's in your glass.

No pressure, no exam — just an approachable, hands-on introduction led by people who know and love this land.

Stillness

A Guided Sound Bath

Somewhere in the middle of the week, the pace slows all the way down. A guided sound bath — gongs and singing bowls — gives your nervous system permission to fully exhale.

For many women, it's one of the most memorable moments of the retreat: no agenda, no conversation, just sound and stillness.

On Foot

Guided Walking Tours

We wander the cobblestone streets of Sainte-Foy-la-Grande and the surrounding villages with a local guide, learning the history behind the architecture, the markets, and the rhythms of daily life in this part of France.

It's an easy, unhurried way to feel like a local rather than a tourist — even if it's your first time in Bordeaux.
